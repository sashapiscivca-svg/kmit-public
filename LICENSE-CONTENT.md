## How the content was made, and its accuracy

- **How it was written.** The lessons and tasks were drafted with AI tools and checked by automated "blind" answer-key verification.
- **Review status.** They have **not yet been reviewed by human subject methodologists**, and they may contain factual, linguistic or methodological errors.
- **Draft label.** Until review, every unit has the status `draft` (`review.status` in the packs). The teacher's lesson library shows this label; the children's screens do not.
- **Copying.** The content is written for Kmit. Textbook texts are not copied.
- **Reporting errors.** Please report errors through the in-app "Повідомити про помилку в завданні" (Report a problem with this task) button or a GitHub issue using the "Content error" template.
