# Kmit Privacy Policy

> **Preliminary version.** This text has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer; we will update it before the app is published in an app store. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

## 1. Who is responsible
Kmit is published by Oleksandr Shynkarenko (a private individual), contact: <sasha.piscivca@gmail.com> ("we").

## 2. In short
- Everything you or your child do in Kmit is stored **only on your device**, encrypted.
- Kmit **sends nothing to the internet**: the app has no network permission, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no payments.
- We **cannot see** your data and cannot recover or delete it for you: it exists only on your device.

## 3. Data the app handles
On the device, inside an encrypted profile: a **name or nickname** chosen by the user (it can be invented; no real surname is needed) and the hero's look; **learning progress** (topics practised, results, reviews, badges, notes the user writes); **settings** (text size, accessibility, time limits, grade); **protection** (PIN verification data; a recovery code shown once). A teacher profile additionally stores the grade, a list of generated worksheets (topics, type, seed, date) and lesson bookmarks. A teacher profile contains **no pupil data**.

The app does **not** collect: surname, address, date of birth, photos, voice, contacts, location, advertising or device identifiers, or the list of other apps.

## 4. Sharing
The app does not transmit anything to us or to third parties. Data leaves the device **only when the user creates a file and shares it themselves**: a PDF sheet for a teacher, a notes PDF, a thank-you card, a profile backup (encrypted with a passphrase), a recovery-code sheet, printed worksheets. Who receives such a file is the user's or the adult's decision; we cannot see it. A child's data is **never passed to a teacher digitally**: a child can only show a printed sheet.

## 5. Children
Kmit is meant for children's learning. A grown-up decides together with the child whether to use it, create a profile and show printed sheets. The app does not ask a child for personal data, shows no ads, has no contact with other users and offers no purchases. An "For the adult" screen is shown before a child's profile is created.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Data is kept while the profile exists on the device. You can **delete the profile and all its data** at any time: "Time and protection" then "Delete profile" (the profile name must be typed to confirm). Deletion is irreversible. Uninstalling the app also erases the data. Files the user saved themselves must be deleted separately.

## 7. Security
The profile is encrypted on the device (SQLCipher; the key is derived from the PIN). After several wrong PIN attempts sign-in is delayed. The recovery code is needed if the PIN is forgotten; without both, the data cannot be recovered, even by us. Please use a screen lock on the device.

## 8. Your rights
You can view your data in the app at any time, change it, make a backup and delete the profile. Because we neither receive nor store your data, requests for access or deletion amount to these actions on your device. Questions: <sasha.piscivca@gmail.com>.

## 9. Legal basis
We follow Ukraine's Law "On Personal Data Protection" (No. 2297-VI), the principles of the GDPR (minimisation, purpose limitation, security) and COPPA-like practices for children. Whether the GDPR applies to a purely local app is not clear-cut; we follow its principles either way. We have not yet obtained a legal opinion on this question.

## 10. Content and its review
Learning content was drafted with AI tools and **has not yet been reviewed by subject methodologists**, so it may contain mistakes. In the teacher's lesson library such material is marked as a draft; the children's screens do not show this label. If you notice a mistake in an exercise, use the in-app "Повідомити про помилку в завданні" (Report a problem with this task) button (a file is created on your device and sent nowhere) or write to us.

## 11. Changes
If the app ever starts transmitting data (for example, downloading courses from the internet), we will update this policy beforehand, tell you in the app and update the Google Play declarations.
